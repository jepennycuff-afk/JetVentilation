What is HFJV?

High-frequency jet ventilation delivers small tidal volumes at 150 cycles/min. In IR procedures, it minimizes target organ movement without requiring breath holds, improving accuracy and reducing radiation time during ablations.

🚫
Relative Contraindications
  • Tracheal stenosis
  • Pulmonary hypertension
  • Pulmonary fibrosis
  • Severe COPD

Setup Checklist
Complete all steps before initiating jet ventilation
SETUP PROGRESS 0 / 14
📞
Pre-Room (6:30 AM)
  • Text IR Anesthesia Tech (Amy / Jerry) to set up Monsoon Jet and TcCO₂ monitor
    Send at 6:30 AM
  • Confirm patient orientation with IR team (head or feet to scanner)
    Determines jet placement and TcCO₂ position
  • Verify 2 PIVs — one for our use, one for IR contrast
    20g or larger, above wrist preferred
🛠️
ETT & Adapter Prep
  • Select 8.0 ETT (minimum)
    Larger ETT required for adequate jet delivery
  • Attach swivel adapter/catheter assembly to ETT
    Adjust pressure monitor line tip 1–2 cm proximal to Murphy eye
  • Apply tape in "flag" formation at swivel adapter to secure position
  • Remove swivel adapter assembly and set aside until post-intubation
    Do not attach until after patient is intubated
📡
TcCO₂ Monitor Setup
  • Place Sentec monitor at foot of bed (visible from control room)
  • Attach TcCO₂ probe immediately after moving patient to procedure table
    Needs 2–10 min to calibrate. Remove both adhesive covers. Apply contact gel drops (1–2 drops).
  • Note baseline difference between TcCO₂ and ETCO₂ before initiating jet
⚙️
Jet Ventilator Initial Settings
  • F (Frequency): 150 cpm
    Do not adjust after setting
  • DP (Driving Pressure): 18 psi — range 10–30 (↑ for high BMI)
    Primary adjustment knob for CO₂ management
  • IT (Inspiratory Time): 35% — range 30–45%
  • FiO₂: 50% | Climatization: 3 or 4 | PP Alarm: 24 | PIP Alarm: 28

CO₂ Management
High TcCO₂ (hypoventilation)
↑ DP by 1–2 psi · Discuss with Anesthesia & IR Faculty before any change after initial scan
Low TcCO₂ (hyperventilation)
↓ DP by 1–2 psi
Hypoxia
↑ FiO₂ · ↑ IT by 5% · ↑ Vent flow 0.5–1 LPM (creates PEEP) · ↑ DP 2 psi

End of Case Steps
  1. IR confirms jet no longer needed after ablation complete
  2. Switch to conventional ventilation
  3. Hyper- or hypoventilate to normalize ETCO₂ to baseline
  4. Perform 1–2 recruitment maneuvers to treat atelectasis
  5. Return TcCO₂ sensor to docking compartment on Sentec monitor
  6. Alert anesthesia tech to remove equipment
